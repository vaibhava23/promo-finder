Promo Explorer

2Score every momentHow strong an opener is it (hook)? How badly would you need to know what happens next (cliffhanger)?
3Try ~1,400 cutsAll 20–60 s, all cut between lines, never mid-sentence. Code guarantees this.
4Rank & planBest first, no near-duplicates, then a posting plan: early free episodes first.
