The best ad clips in

Apps like this one grow by posting short clips of their shows on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube. This tool read every episode's script and picked the clips most likely to make a stranger stop scrolling and start watching. Click any clip below to play it and see why it was chosen.

What makes a good ad clip

Grab attentionThe first 3 seconds need a shock: a slap, a reveal, a cruel line.Otherwise people scroll past.
Build the drama20 to 60 seconds in total, cut straight from the show, easy to follow with no backstory.No editing tricks, just the right start and end.
Stop on a questionEnd at the worst possible moment, before anything is resolved.The viewer has to know what happens.
…the rest is in the appWhat happens next is cut off.That's why people tap and install.

All picks

Words used on this page
Ad clip (promo)
A 20 to 60 second piece of the show posted on social media to attract new viewers.
Hook
The first 3 seconds. It has to shock or intrigue someone who is scrolling past.
Cliffhanger
The moment the clip stops: something unresolved, so the viewer needs to know what happens next.
Jump cut
Two pieces of the show joined together, with a slow part in between left out.
Post first / next / later
The posting plan. Clips from early free episodes go first; clips that reveal late twists are shown later, to people already watching.
Makes sense alone
Whether someone who has never seen the show can follow the clip without knowing the characters.
How it decides
It scores every line of every episode (how shocking, how unresolved, how central to the episode), tries about 1,400 possible clips that start and stop cleanly between lines, and keeps the best varied set. A person still approves the final picks.
